### Executive Summary

Developed as part of the **Deutsche Bank Hackathon 2026**, **Eurorisk Deep Mind** addresses the European banking sector's 2026–2028 supervisory priorities: navigating geopolitical volatility, cyber resilience, and strict compliance with the **Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)**.

### Core Architectural Pillars

- **Autonomous Multi-Agent AI**: Deploys multi-agent AI systems utilizing Vector Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to dynamically monitor complex credit risks, Commercial Real Estate (CRE) loan exposures, and sovereign bond mark-to-market losses.
- **DORA & Operational Resilience**: Automates real-time tracking of critical ICT vendor dependencies, third-party concentration risks, and regulatory compliance standards across European banking jurisdictions.
- **Proactive Shock Simulations**: Replaces static quarterly reporting with daily, automated macro-economic and geopolitical shock simulations to proactively forecast bank liquidity and capital adequacy ratios.
- **Cloud-Native Scalability**: Engineered with enterprise-grade Python backend architectures, containerized microservices, and interactive executive dashboards for Chief Risk Officers (CROs).

### Technology Stack

- **Backend**: Python, FastAPI, Pydantic
- **AI / LLMs**: LangGraph, Vector RAG, Ollama / Vertex AI
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Cloud Run, Docker
- **Regulatory Frameworks**: DORA, EBA Supervisory Guidelines
