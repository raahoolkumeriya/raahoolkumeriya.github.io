Eurorisk Deep Mind – AI-Driven CRO Command Center

Developed as part of the Deutsche Bank Hackathon 2026, Eurorisk Deep Mind addresses the European banking sector's 2026–2028 supervisory priorities: navigating geopolitical volatility, cyber resilience, and strict compliance with the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA).

Solution Highlights

Autonomous AI Agents

Deploys multi-agent AI systems utilizing Vector Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to dynamically monitor complex credit risks, Commercial Real Estate (CRE) loan exposures, and sovereign bond mark-to-market (MTM) losses.

DORA & Operational Resilience

Automates real-time tracking of critical ICT vendor dependencies, third-party concentration risks, and regulatory compliance standards across European banking jurisdictions.

Proactive Intelligence

Replaces static regulatory reporting with daily, automated macro-economic and geopolitical shock simulations to proactively forecast bank liquidity and capital ratios.

Scalable Cloud Stack

Built with enterprise-grade Python backend architectures, cloud-native deployments, and interactive executive dashboards for Chief Risk Officers (CROs).
